Suraj Sharma

about me · publications

Hi there, thank you for visiting. I'm a recent graduate from Ashoka University with an advanced major in Computer Science. Before Ashoka, I completed two years of foundational education in natural science under the BS-MS program at IISER Kolkata, and later joined Ashoka due to my growing interest in computer science.

In Computer Science, I am broadly interested in computer networks and network security. My recent work focuses on network security, particularly client deanonymization attacks in NAT-enabled Wi-Fi networks.

Beyond computers, I've always been drawn to the humanities. I have some experience with Hindi writing and occasionally enjoy reading literature and exploring philosophical ideas, especially those with existential themes.

I'm also passionate about teaching. I have taught Science and Mathematics to students of classes K–8 at Ek Pehal, IISER Kolkata and at Neev, the community engagement club of Ashoka University.

Over time, I've grown interested in civic issues related to the internet - privacy, digital freedom, and global inequality in access to information. I'm hoping to do something meaningful in these areas in the near future.
